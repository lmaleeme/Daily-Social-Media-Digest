Marketers evaluating Reddit as an ad channel should expect a smaller but higher-intent audience, and should reassess campaign goals around engagement quality rather than raw impression volume, while watching upcoming earnings calls for concrete metrics on how this transition affects user counts, ad revenue, and advertiser demand on the platform.
